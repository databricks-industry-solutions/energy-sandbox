DIGITAL TWIN + GENIE + MULTI-AGENT APPROVAL SUPERVISOR FOR MATURE-FIELD PRODUCTION
Production Optimizer combines a 3D digital twin, governed natural-language access via AI/BI Genie, and a five-specialist Recommendation Approval Supervisor that fans out across Foundation Model API, UC SQL, and Vector Search to synthesise APPROVE / APPROVE-WITH-MODS / DEFER / REJECT for any optimization recommendation.
◆ Business Value
  • Catch decline early with Arps fits + EUR + remaining-reserves per well
  • Approve high-impact recs faster — five specialists run in parallel; verdict in seconds
  • Audit every decision — every supervisor verdict logged with cited evidence
  • Self-service insight — engineers ask Genie in plain English, not SQL
  • One governance plane — Unity Catalog covers tables, models, queries, and audit
◆ Challenges Addressed
  • Decision drift — engineers stitch 5+ tools per recommendation; nothing tied together
  • Manual interpretation — decline curves and rec impact reviewed by hand
  • Slow approval cycles — sign-off chains stretch days; capex bleeds
  • No precedent context — past recommendations live in spreadsheets, not the workflow
  • Genie + Supervisor in one app — chat and verdict surfaces unified, not two products
