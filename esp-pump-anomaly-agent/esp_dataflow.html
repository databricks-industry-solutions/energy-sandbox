1 - Ingest
25 ESP wells stream pressure, temperature, vibration and current into pump_telemetry as high-frequency Delta.
2 - Refine
latest_reading_per_well snapshots the newest reading per well; pump_features aggregates per well-hour for ML.
3 - Detect
Threshold rules (1200-2400 psi, motor temp 250°F) and 3σ anomaly detection flag at-risk wells.
4 - Predict
A classifier scores each well-hour and writes pump_failure_predictions with a failure probability.
5 - Advise
A Mosaic AI agent calls 6 live SQL tools to investigate a well and recommend actions.
6 - Visualize
This dashboard unifies fleet KPIs, well-level telemetry, and the AI investigator.
